## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
Password Disk is an offline-first password manager. Your vault lives on your phone, encrypted with a key derived from your master password — no account needed, no network required.

■ ENCRYPTED AND LOCAL BY DEFAULT
· The master password is verified on-device; it is never stored in plaintext and never uploaded
· Passwords, usernames, notes and URLs are AES-encrypted before they touch the local database
· Release builds block screenshots and screen recording, so your secrets stay off screen shares
· Copied passwords are flagged as sensitive and wiped from the clipboard after 60 seconds

■ FIND ANY PASSWORD IN SECONDS
· Live search across titles, usernames, URLs and notes
· Category chips filter the list with one tap
· Open an entry to see details, copy the username or password, or jump straight to the login page

■ BUILT-IN PASSWORD GENERATOR
· Choose length and character sets — upper case, lower case, digits, symbols
· Live strength meter shows whether the result is actually strong enough

■ A DEDICATED 2FA TAB
· Paste a Base32 secret or scan a QR code to import
· 30-second TOTP codes with a circular countdown
· Codes roll over automatically — no manual refresh
· Copy a code with one tap, protected by the same clipboard rules

■ OPTIONAL CLOUD BACKUP
· Nothing goes online unless you turn it on; backups are encrypted on-device before upload
· The server cannot read your passwords
· Restore your vault on a new phone by signing in
· Leave it off and the app runs fully offline with zero network requests

■ WHAT WE DON'T DO
· No ads, no splash pop-ups, no paywall
· No access to contacts, SMS, location or device identifiers
· No analytics, tracking or profiling SDKs
· No permissions beyond the ones the features need

■ ONE THING YOU SHOULD KNOW
A lost master password cannot be recovered. That is the price of end-to-end encryption — we do not hold your key.
Please keep your master password somewhere you can find it again.

■ PERMISSIONS
· Camera — only for scanning 2FA QR codes. Frames are decoded on-device and never stored or uploaded. You can deny it and type the secret manually instead.
· Network — only for cloud backup you explicitly start. Without it, the app is fully offline.

Privacy policy: see the link at the bottom of this store listing.
```
